How it works. Open the kit folder once; the tool reads the base tiles, the overlays and every object folder (your own PNGs too). A map is built in layers: one base tile, one overlay laid on it, then the objects. Pick a tile, an overlay and a theme (or leave them on Random), press Generate (every click makes a new map). Trees, rocks and mushrooms are placed in natural groups (a giant mushroom with small ones around it, a skirt of bushes around trees and rocks), roads and water stay free. The same seed always gives the same map. Nothing is uploaded: the files stay on your computer.
